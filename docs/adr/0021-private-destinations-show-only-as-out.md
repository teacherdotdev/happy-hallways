# Private destinations show only as "Out"

The door shows who is out and where (ADR 0003), so a returning student finds
their own name. But some trips are nobody else's business: the Nurse, the
Counselor, the office. So a destination can be Private. Wherever students can
see, a trip there shows only as "Out": on the kiosk's tiles and notices, and in
the counts on students' devices, where Private trips are folded into one "Out",
never named or drawn with their icon. The teacher always sees the real
destination, on Home, in Needs you, in History and in the CSV.

## Consequences

- A Private destination has no Pass Limit and no Line, under either way of
  limiting: a line would show the room who is waiting to go there. A student it
  would stop for another reason (No-Pass Time, a used-up Pass Allowance) asks the
  teacher instead.
- The student going there still picks it from the list of destinations, and
  their own claimed device shows them where they are.
- The kiosk's approval says "you can go" rather than naming the place.
